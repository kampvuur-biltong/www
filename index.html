Do you work on a consignment basis?

We do not operate on a consignment basis. We provide samples for your consideration, and all stock is supplied on a direct-sale basis.

How do you ensure quality?

Our commitment is fresh, premium-quality biltong and professional service. If any product within its expiry period experiences an unexpected change in colour, fading or spoilage, contact us so the issue can be reviewed and resolved promptly.

What is the best way to store Kampvuur Biltong?

Biltong should be stored in a cool, dry, well-ventilated place. Keep it away from direct sunlight and moisture, and refrigerate softer biltong when appropriate to preserve freshness for longer.
